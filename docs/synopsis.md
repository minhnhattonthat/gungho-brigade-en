# Synopsis (built from the script as it is translated; extend per stage)

Setting: a terraformed Mars. The Earth Forces (地球軍) hold it; an unknown, non-human enemy force of machines appears.
Machine legs (マシンレッグ) = walking mecha, used both for war and for the sport Rug Battle (ラグバトル).

Who is who
- Bizley Leonard (ビズリィ), "the Gungho Kid": hot-headed, loud, rough speech (ぜ/ねぇ), leader-ish of the crew.
- Chavez Shelby (シャベス): older, terse, ex-soldier feel; hunts the "Chrome Devil" (クロームデビル, an enemy ace).
- Brains = Ralph Lyall (ブレインズ): polite (です/ます), analytical, dry sarcasm.
- Betty Davis (ベティ): the crew's caring older-sister type; drives the Hercules support vehicle.
- Daibutsu = Reggie Simmons (ダイブツ): big, gentle, few words, childlike drawl (〜よぉ).
- Maiko Hinowa (マイコ): sharp-tongued, tomboyish, fond of drink; spars with Bizley.
- John Smith (ジョン): a boy who knows the enemy "isn't human"; wants to reach New Brown.

Stages so far
- s00 tutorial: a short machine-leg chase (prologue).
- s01 first battle.
- s02 Rug Battle match; the crew helps John vs. thugs, then meets him at a bar; he asks to go to New Brown;
  introductions (names above).
- s03 John at the Governor's Office warns a guard that "they're coming" and the enemy isn't human; the crew,
  registered by Bizley as G-KAHP ("Gungho Kid And His Pals", Maiko hates it), plays the Pavonis Cup.
- s04 the match vs. the thugs' "Crushers" turns into a live-fire brawl; security steps in; G-KAHP disqualified.
- s05 the enemy attacks Artsutanov (orbital/space elevator, autonomous zone, Governor Barney Phelps); Phobos is
  being dropped onto it. The crew stages a diversion so evacuation trailers escape (Brains: the enemy is built to beat
  Earth tech: jamming, anti-laser armor, shock attacks; Rug Battle machines resist shocks). Chavez sees the Chrome
  Devil and wants to fight; Brains drags him away.
- s06 Outer Planets Union briefing to the Federation Ambassador: Operation Dunkirk will evacuate all 2 million
  Martians; the Junkies descend from "Junk", alien self-replicating terraformers found on Mars 32 years ago; a
  simulation shows Earth overrun in 2 months. Vice Admiral Raymond Tovey asks Earth to take the refugees. Captain
  Esmeralda falsified the data (the truth would make Earth abandon Mars). The crew visits Oldman (Chavez's old CO,
  retired brigadier general) at Chita; he refuses to hunt the Chrome Devil. Betty recites Wordsworth's "My Heart Leaps
  Up". Esmeralda drops in by LST (20% success odds, her own plan; Earth codes are useless vs. the Junkies); the crew and
  Oldman rescue her.

  Dragoon leads, no firing until the charge. Oldman shields Bizley and is hurt.

  "transmitter". The crew guards the last shuttle and the mass driver is blown; Oldman dies (s11 mentions it).
- s10 The crew misses the last Earth Express ("May God protect those left behind").
- s11 Aboard ship: John's father is at Fort McAuliffe (Arcadia evacuation failed). Maiko finds a machine-leg simulator.

  Technical Major William "Billy" Neeson) tells Daibutsu about the cryosleep capsule shortfall; Union troops drop to
  Mars to free up capsules.
